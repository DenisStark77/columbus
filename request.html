Tell us what you need.

One form for everything: the comparable sales for a lot, a call, a visit, a night in the guesthouse, a lot report, or listing a lot you own. We reply personally, usually within a few days, and agree the price and the way to pay then.

  • Five comparable sales for a lotfree
  • 15-minute orientation call$50
  • Half-day visitfrom $150
  • Full day with a mealfrom $250
  • Night in the guesthouseon request
  • Lot reportfrom $200
  • Listing your lotfree within the comparable range

Prices are guides; the $50 of the call counts toward any later step. Payment is agreed after we have talked — transfer, SINPE, cash. Nothing is charged on this site.

We write only to the email you give and only about your request. Ask at any time and we delete what you sent.
