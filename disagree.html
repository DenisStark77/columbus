The estimate

Not convinced by a range?

Tell us the lot and what you think is wrong — a recent sale we missed, a feature that changes everything, a number that simply looks off. We read every message and correct what is wrong.

What helps us most: the lot number; what you think the lot is worth and why; a sale you know of — year and price, no names needed; and an email so we can answer.

The range is for a standard 5,000 m² lot, land only, from five comparable closed sales. The method page explains every step and what is left out.
