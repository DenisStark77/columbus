What the registry says about the market.

Everything below comes from the public extracts of the 212 parcels of Columbus Heights, read on the date shown. The samples are small; the number of sales sits next to every figure for a reason.

191 of the 212 parcels measure between 5,000 and 6,000 m²; nine are larger than 7,000 m² and four smaller than 5,000 m². Lots 18–24 are not separate parcels — they are registered as one 5.7 ha parcel together with lot 17. Lots 63 and 64 were merged into another parcel in 2007.

Purchases per year

Purchases are compraventa acts in the history of the 212 parcels, excluding the original 2006 subdivision sale. The series starts in 2019: earlier years are few and the history is less complete the further back it goes. The 2019–2021 peak includes many transfers declared at cadastral value; the drop from late 2024 is the period without grid electricity.

Declared purchase prices in US dollars

Only purchases declared in US dollars by the current owner count here, one per lot. About half of all purchases are declared in colones at or near cadastral value — around ₡5 to 5.4 million — and are left out as uninformative. Declared prices can be lower than what was actually paid.

What the registry records

Counts of parcels by kind of entry, on the snapshot date. A parcel can appear in more than one row. Which parcels these are is on the map, lot by lot, and in the paid report, entry by entry.

Parcels with no entries at all, with a mortgage or tax lien, in foreclosure proceedings, with a preventive note, or with transfers blocked by a court order.

How to read this page

  • Prices are the "estimación o precio" declared in the deed; they can understate the real price.
  • Dollar statistics use only current-owner purchases declared in US dollars, one per lot; earlier resales of the same lot are not in the data.
  • Purchases declared in colones at or near cadastral value are excluded from price statistics.
  • Since November 2024 the community has had no grid electricity. An agreement with ICE is paid and works are under way as of November 2026; the connection is not there yet.
  • A registry check is not legal advice. Any purchase needs a Costa Rican lawyer.
